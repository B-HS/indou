Everything AltTab did, and more.

⌨

Custom shortcuts

Record any modifier + key. Run multiple trigger profiles — all windows, current app, and more.

▦

Every real window

ScreenCaptureKit + Accessibility, across Spaces and displays, including minimized and fullscreen. Background-only apps stay out.

⇄

Grid navigation

Tab to cycle, arrow or vim keys to roam the 2D grid, release to focus. Auto-scrolls to your selection.

✕

Act on windows

Close, minimize, fullscreen, or quit right from the switcher. Drag-select or ⌘-click to act on many at once.

⌕

Instant search

Six-tier fuzzy matching, plus Korean initial-consonant search. Type a few letters, land on the window.

✦

Smooth & quiet

120 Hz transitions you can toggle off, a non-activating overlay that never steals focus, and full localization.

Built for your hands.

Hold the trigger, the switcher appears. Everything happens on the keyboard.
